Butterfly Pendulums

Every pendulum starts a tiny fraction of a degree further out than the one before. One arm and they stay in step forever. Two arms and the tiny gap grows until they fly apart.

A double pendulum is chaotic: its motion is fully set by the laws of physics, yet a starting difference too small to measure doubles again and again until it takes over. That is the butterfly effect, and it's why weather forecasts break down after about two weeks. A simple pendulum isn't chaotic, so the same tiny gap stays tiny.
