You push the small block; it pushes the big one. Both blocks start moving only when your push beats friction on both: F ≥ μ·g·(m + M). Once moving, the small block passes on F × M ÷ (m + M), so the heavier the big block, the closer that gets to your full push. Friction changes how fast they speed up, not that share.
