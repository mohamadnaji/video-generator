Slingshot Planets

Press anywhere in space and pull back like a slingshot. The dashed line shows where the moon will go. Let go to launch it.

Gravity pulls a moon toward the star; its sideways speed carries it past. At the circular speed v = √(GM ÷ r) the two balance and it circles forever. Slower and it falls inward, faster and its path stretches into an ellipse, and at √2 times circular speed (escape speed) it never comes back. A giant planet adds small tugs that can wreck an orbit that was fine without it.
